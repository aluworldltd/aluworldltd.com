How it works

From first visit to final check

Timing depends on measurements, material availability, fabrication and access. We'll explain the expected schedule with your quote.

  1. Site visit

    We look at the surfaces to be covered, how they meet windows, doors and roof edges, and access for the work.

  2. Measuring

    We measure each surface, column and soffit, and note what the panels will be fixed to.

  3. Layout & quote

    We plan where panels and joints fall, go through finish options, and quote with the expected schedule.

  4. Installation

    Panels and trim are fixed, joints and edges are finished, and the area is cleaned up.

Common questions

Before you call

More answers in our full list of questions, or call (647) 504-4338.

Can existing columns be wrapped?

Yes. We wrap round and square columns. We measure the column, check what it's made of and its condition, and plan how the wrap meets the ceiling and the ground.

What finishes are available?

It depends on the panel system and the project. We'll go through the colours and finishes available for your job when we quote.

How are dimensions and joints planned?

From site measurements. We measure every surface, then lay out the panels so joints line up with corners, windows and other edges where possible, and so trim finishes cleanly at the ends.
