How it works

From first visit to final check

Timing depends on measurements, material availability, fabrication and access. We'll explain the expected schedule with your quote.

  1. Site visit

    We look at the layout, where people walk, where doors are needed and how the space will be used.

  2. Measuring

    We measure heights and widths at several points along each run, check the floor and ceiling for level, and note the surfaces the glass connects to.

  3. Options & quote

    Glass, framing, doors and hardware, with the expected schedule.

  4. Installation

    Channels or frames are fixed, glass and doors are installed and adjusted, and the area is cleaned up.

Common questions

Before you call

More answers in our full list of questions, or call (647) 504-4338.

What framing options do you offer?

The photos on this page show black-framed glass walls and glass with minimal framing. We'll go through the framing options that suit your space, doors and budget when we quote.

How is the glass measured?

On site. We measure heights and widths at several points along each wall, because floors and ceilings are rarely perfectly level, and note what the glass will be fixed to at the floor, walls and ceiling.

What should I expect for sound privacy?

Glass partitions cut down noise compared with an open office, but they aren't soundproof, and sound can pass through door gaps and around the edges. If privacy matters for the room, tell us how it will be used and we'll explain the options.

Do you install mirrors too?

Yes. We measure and install wall mirrors in any shape for salons, studios and homes. See custom mirrors.
