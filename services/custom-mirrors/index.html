What we do

What's included

Every wall and mirror is assessed when we measure, so the mirror fits the space.

  • Any shape

    Rectangles and squares, round and oval, arched, and custom shapes cut to a template.

  • Mirror walls

    Full mirror walls for salons, studios and homes.

  • Edges

    Polished flat edges or bevelled edges.

  • Framed or frameless

    Framed mirrors, or frameless mirrors held with clips or channels.

  • Cutouts and holes

    Cutouts for outlets and switches, and holes for faucets, lights and mounting hardware.

  • LED lighting and defoggers

    Mirrors with built-in LED lighting or defoggers.

How it works

From first visit to final check

Timing depends on measurements, material availability, fabrication and access. We'll explain the expected schedule with your quote.

  1. Site visit

    We look at the wall, what's around it (outlets, switches, faucets and lights) and how the mirror will be used.

  2. Measuring

    We measure the wall, mark outlets, switches and fixtures that need cutouts, and check the surface the mirror will be fixed to.

  3. Options & quote

    Shape, edge, framing, cutouts and lighting, with the expected schedule.

  4. Installation

    The mirror is fixed with suitable hardware, cutouts are lined up, and the area is cleaned up.

Common questions

Before you call

More answers in our full list of questions, or call (647) 504-4338.

What shapes can you make?

Rectangles and squares, round and oval, arched, full mirror walls, and custom shapes cut to a template.

Can you cut holes for outlets and switches?

Yes. We measure and mark outlets, switches, faucets and lights on site, and the mirror is cut to match.

Can a mirror have built-in lighting?

Yes. We offer mirrors with built-in LED lighting and defoggers.

Is my wall suitable for a mirror?

We check each wall when we measure and recommend how the mirror should be mounted.
