Common questions

Before you call

More answers in our full list of questions, or call (647) 504-4338.

How much does a job cost?

Every project is priced individually. We come to your property and take measurements for free, then give you a quote based on the size, glass, materials, hardware and access. There's no obligation.

How long does a job take?

It depends on the job. Smaller jobs usually take about 5–15 business days, and bigger projects about 15–20 business days. We'll give you a timeline for your project with your quote.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. The warranty depends on the type of job. Ask us when you request your quote and we'll give you the exact coverage for your project.

Can you come and look at the job first?

Yes. We offer free, no-obligation site visits. Select a free site visit in the quote form and optionally tell us your preferred day. We'll contact you to arrange and confirm the visit.
