How it works

From first visit to final check

Timing depends on measurements, material availability, fabrication and access. We'll explain the expected schedule with your quote.

  1. Photos first

    Send photos of the bathroom and tell us the type of opening: walk-in, alcove, corner or tub.

  2. Measuring

    We measure once the walls, tile and shower base are finished, checking the walls for plumb, the base for level and the space for the door to open.

  3. Options & quote

    Glass, door style and hardware options for your layout, with the expected schedule.

  4. Installation

    The glass and hardware are installed, the door is adjusted to open and close properly, and seals are fitted.

Common questions

Before you call

More answers in our full list of questions, or call (647) 504-4338.

When should the shower be measured?

Once the tile and shower base are finished. The glass is made to fit the finished surfaces, so measuring before the tile is in can lead to a poor fit.

Which way can the door open?

It depends on the space outside the shower. A hinged door needs room to swing clear of the vanity, toilet and bathroom door. Where there isn't room, a sliding door can be an option. We check clearance when we measure.

What affects the options?

The shape of the opening, the wall and ceiling height, whether the walls are straight, where the shower controls are, the space for the door to open, and your glass and hardware choices.

How do I look after shower glass?

Squeegee or wipe the glass after showers to reduce water spots, and clean it with a non-abrasive glass cleaner. Avoid scouring pads and harsh chemicals on the glass, hardware and seals.
