What we do

What's included

We come to you, and there's no minimum: we'll come out for a single screen.

  • Window screens

    Torn or loose mesh replaced, and bent or broken frames repaired or replaced.

  • Patio door screens

    Sliding patio door screens with torn mesh, damaged frames or worn parts.

  • Hinged screen doors

    Swinging screen doors with torn mesh or damaged frames.

  • Retractable screens

How it works

From first visit to final check

Timing depends on measurements, material availability, fabrication and access. We'll explain the expected schedule with your quote.

  1. Tell us what's damaged

    Send a photo of the screen and tell us how many need work and where they are.

  2. On-site visit

    We come to your home or building, check the mesh, frame and parts on each screen, and measure any that need replacing.

  3. Quote

    We explain what can be repaired and what needs replacing, with the expected schedule.

  4. Repair or replace

    Mesh is replaced, frames and parts are repaired or replaced, and each screen is refitted and checked.

Common questions

Before you call

More answers in our full list of questions, or call (647) 504-4338.

Do I need to bring my screens in?

No. We come to your home or building and work on the screens there.

Is there a minimum?

No. We'll come out for a single screen.

Should I repair the screen or replace it?

If the frame is straight and the parts work, replacing the mesh is usually enough. If the frame is bent, broken or missing parts, a new screen made to fit may be the better option. We'll explain what we find.

Do you have stronger mesh for pets?

Yes. Pet-resistant mesh is heavier than standard mesh and holds up better to scratching and pushing.
