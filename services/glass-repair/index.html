Common questions

Before you call

More answers in our full list of questions, or call (647) 504-4338.

Can just the glass be replaced?

Often, yes. If the frame, sash and hardware are in good condition, we can usually replace only the broken glass or the sealed unit. If the frame is damaged or worn out, we'll show you what we found and explain the options before you decide. If the whole unit needs replacing, see windows & doors.

What causes fog between the panes?

Double- and triple-pane windows are sealed units. When the seal around the edge fails, moist air gets between the panes and condenses, which shows up as fog or haze you can't wipe off because it's inside the glass. Replacing the sealed unit fixes it, and the frame can often stay.

What photos should I send?

A close-up of the damage, a photo of the whole window or door from inside (and outside if you can), and a photo of any label or stamp in the corner of the glass. Add rough sizes and your city or postal code. You can attach up to 3 photos in our quote request form.

Do you replace balcony and condo glass?

Yes. We repair and replace balcony glass, condo windows and glass on high-rise buildings, including work at height.
