Common questions

Before you call

More answers in our full list of questions, or call (647) 504-4338.

Do you do emergency repairs?

Yes. For broken glass or damaged aluminum, such as windows, doors, frames and storefronts, we come the same day, safely remove the damaged glass or parts and, if needed, cover the opening until the replacement is ready. For urgent help, call (647) 504-4338.

Do you work on condo balconies and high-rise buildings?

Yes. We repair and replace balcony glass, condo windows and glass on high-rise buildings, including work at height.

How much does a job cost?

Every project is priced individually. We come to your property and take measurements for free, then give you a quote based on the size, glass, materials, hardware and access. There's no obligation.

Do you do repairs, or only new installations?

Both. We repair cracked or fogged windows, balcony glass, doors, screens, hardware and failed seals, and we install new windows, doors, railings, shower doors, storefronts, glass partitions and sunrooms.
